## Primary research checked for this pass

- [Tourism Authority of Thailand: Hua Lamphong photographs, nominal map pin and MRT BL28 access](https://www.thailandtravel.or.jp/hua-lamphong-station/). The linked pin is 13.73922, 100.5167605; it is a nominal place pin, not a surveyed entrance.
- [BEM: Hua Lamphong exits and mainline-station connection at exit 2](https://metro.bemplc.co.th/Line-Maps?Line=2&Station=28). Mainline station and underground MRT entrance remain distinct.
- [Nendo: Siam Discovery exterior redevelopment](https://www.nendo.jp/en/works/siam-discovery/).
- [Siam Paragon operator](https://www.siamparagon.co.th/about), [CentralWorld operator/map](https://www.centralworld.co.th/en), [Terminal 21 Asok operator](https://www.terminal21.co.th/asok/contactus/en).
- [Shangri-La street arrival and Saphan Taksin relationship](https://www.shangri-la.com/bangkok/shangrila/about/map-directions/), [Peninsula riverside property](https://www.peninsula.com/en/bangkok/5-star-luxury-hotel-riverside), [Hilton property](https://www.hilton.com/en/hotels/bkkhitw-millennium-hilton-bangkok/).
- [Mandarin Oriental gallery](https://www.mandarinoriental.com/en/bangkok/chao-phraya-river/gallery), [Four Seasons location](https://www.fourseasons.com/bangkok/getting-here/), [Capella](https://capellahotels.com/en/capella-bangkok), [Rosewood location](https://www.rosewoodhotels.com/en/bangkok/overview/location). Operator photos without a verified redistribution licence are linked, not copied.
- Existing primary geographic/architectural citations are retained in [PR29 research](../../master-plan/references.md) and [architectural-source inventory](../../real-bangkok-architecture/references.md). A retained citation alone does not mean a building was newly surveyed.
