# Remaining accuracy and completeness work

This pass does not certify the entire city as geographically accurate. Registry entries with no verified geographic coordinate retain `not-verified`; photographs, inferred cardinal axes and authored game-metre dimensions are separate evidence types.

| Priority | Place / system | Remaining issue | Acceptance evidence needed |
|---|---|---|---|

Loha Prasat, the Marble Temple and Holy Rosary Church are not independently represented named landmarks in this checkout. Wat Suthat is modeled in the Giant Swing compound; State Tower is reused as Lebua rather than duplicated. These distinctions are reflected in the registry.
