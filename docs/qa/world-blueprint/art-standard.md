# Premium stylized semi-realism — production standard v1

Recognition, proportion, composition and coherent materials take priority over object count. The two implemented benchmarks are playable generated environments, not concept images. They establish a reusable improvement standard; the entire city is not yet certified to it.

| Tier | Treatment | Review gate |
|---|---|---|
| A — hero | Researched site-specific massing/silhouette, existing named builder, canonical origin/yaw and protected parcel. | Driving/entrance/elevated renderer views against licensed or linked photos; identify missing recognizable features. |
| B — signature | Curated heritage row, civic frontage, commercial corner or station interface supported by shared grammar. | Verify the property before applying a real company name. Generic trade signs must not impersonate it. |
| C — supporting | Existing batched grammar; coherent bay/floor dimensions, front/rear facade, materials and planting. | No blocked roads, floating footings, arbitrary temple roofs or unbounded independent props. |

Yaowarat extends the bespoke facade zone from 19 to 38 existing parcels along the approved one-way curve. Two-to-five-storey facades fit collider massing. Six warm/mineral plaster families, coordinated trim and a composed nine-trade sequence reduce random rainbow/repeated shop treatments. Shared geometry supplies deep shop rooms, pilasters/cornices, upper windows/shutters, balconies, awnings, supported blades, end-wall articulation, thresholds/drainage and selective warm interiors. Small non-solid parapet/gable ornaments retain the existing roof envelope. Existing people, carts, outdoor seating, lamps, cables and lantern crossings remain lightweight systems.

Tha Tien applies the same deep heritage frontage to 42 existing low-rise waterfront parcels, retaining two-storey cream masonry, green joinery, service-side shutters, arched window crowns and continuous pitched tile roofs. Street shops and river service elevations stay distinct. PR33 river edges, commercial density and pier access remain. This is not a measured replica of the complete historic U-shaped market.

These twelve ordinary labels use the existing atlas and licensed OFL Noto fonts. Cmap tests check all rendered characters. Text sits on exterior planes, supported above vehicle clearance. Four inherited real-brand percentile assignments are removed from the Bangkok district generator. Hua Seng Heng remains a sourced inventory record; no real logo is randomly assigned to an arbitrary generated property. Exact branch identity and branding permissions require separate review.

Reuse existing PBR array finishes, rough masonry, restrained metal highlights, cell batching and near/detail/night/coarse distance controls. Keep warm sunset light, selective emissions and readable roads. No global bloom/SSAO, large photographic walls, new animation system or global point-light pass is added. Nighttime contrast still needs art refinement; do not award A from test results.

Retain distinct planting contexts: Sanam Luang tamarind edge, temple shade, Golden Mount landscaping, ornamental park/hotel planting and sparse Chinatown trees. Future canopy improvements must share geometry, vary branching intentionally and prove full-crown road/rail/entrance clearance. This PR does not claim a citywide botanical rollout.
