# Remaining city production sequence

| Phase | Concrete work / dependencies | Acceptance criteria |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Geographic gaps | Resolve null inventory coordinates, named bridge coverage, missing street families, exact entrance bearings and BMA availability/licence. Confirm BTS omissions with operator rather than station-number inference. | Every correction has source, bank, street relationship, confidence and explicit compressed footprint. Unverified items remain warnings. |
| 2. Hotel certification | Oriental, Peninsula, Hilton, Shangri-La, Lebua, Four Seasons, Capella, Anantara, then Okura, Rosewood, Park Hyatt, Grand Hyatt, Dusit and Eastin. Depends on phase 1 bearings. | Intended named model/origin; correct bank, tower/podium/height relationships, entrance/frontage and neighbors; street/river/elevated sunset/night comparisons and spatial checks. |
| 3. Chinatown / Talat Noi | Verified gold shops, Holy Rosary, Grand Postal and Song Wat warehouse corners. | No invented business identity; preserved one-way curve; coherent varied rows; recognizable signature buildings; driven links to station/river. |
| 4. Old Town missing icons | Loha Prasat/Ratchanatdaram, Bowonniwet, Phra Sumen, Benchamabophit and civic compounds. | Approved parcels before infill, identifiable street-level architecture, realistic partial obstruction, PR32–33 relationships preserved. |
| 5. Siam / Ratchaprasong | Mall renovation references, compound sub-place identity, supporting blocks and hotel interfaces. Depends on phase 2. | Discovery/Center/Paragon/CentralWorld order, rail clearance, believable arrivals, resource budget and matched views. |
| 6. Sukhumvit / Silom / Sathorn | Corridor art rollout, One Bangkok/Dusit/towers, QSNCC and station interfaces. | District grammar, skyline hierarchy, continuous playable streets, clear entrances, separate static/traffic journey evidence. |
| 7. Thonburi / north / south | Kalayanamit/Kudi Chin/Santa Cruz, northern parks/Or Tor Kor/central terminal, southern waterfront. | Correct bank/district adjacency, sourced heroes, no disconnected decorative streets, explicit beyond-map exclusions. |
| 8. Physical mobile | Real Safari tests including iPhone 17 Pro Max after scene acceptance. | Device/OS/resolution/quality/thermal state, frame-time distribution, memory and restart/context-loss results. No SwiftShader substitution. |

Cross-cutting: exact parcels/entrances, hotel likeness, richer shop corners, selected vegetation upgrades, night sign/interior contrast and inherited Wat Pho → Hua Lamphong traffic collision frames. Traffic tuning belongs in a focused follow-up unless this PR regresses it.

Every neighborhood rollout must retain frozen geography, actual-renderer before/after driving evidence, source/licence records, honest A–F review, controller travel and resource accounting. Approval guards prevent an art pass from silently undoing geography.
