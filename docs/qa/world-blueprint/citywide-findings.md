# Citywide inspection findings

The survey covers fourteen actual destination arrivals and five additional elevated views, at sunset and night. Chase-camera height/distance use the real renderer. Arrival headings face the model; these are static inspection setups, not moving-route evidence. Benchmark driving views and controller routes are separately documented. No city objects are hidden to improve composition.

The inspected mall arrivals reveal the need for a later entrance/composition pass: Terminal 21 presents a largely blank podium wall; ICONSIAM's near arrival view is dominated by canopy/podium and guideway; Central Embassy's near arrival wall has little identifiable detail. Siam Paragon's name and curved glass are readable but facade depth remains simplistic. These views are retained rather than replaced with flattering elevated shots.

The Palace and Wat Arun retain recognizable roof/spire language, but arrival cameras crop the full silhouettes. Paknam's elevated view shows a distinctive golden Buddha while its surrounding visible urban fabric is sparse. Chatuchak's elevated view exposes simplified park/rail/urban composition. The limited render radius/LOD is not a claim that distant culled areas contain no geometry.

The survey proves that the repeatable capture system observes the live world and exposes uneven quality. It does not certify every district, hotel orientation, entrance or silhouette. No new citywide A grades are awarded. Priorities: (1) source/entrance bearings and named-model checks, (2) hotel/hero massing and arrival composition, (3) coherent district fabric, (4) nuanced night lighting and planting. See the concrete phased roadmap.
