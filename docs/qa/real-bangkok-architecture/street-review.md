# Remaining open-edge audit

The full machine-readable audit lists every remaining open ray in `remaining-edges.json`. The PR24 comparison is `baseline-edge-followup.json`; 67 of the old 151 open positions no longer have an open ray within eight metres, while 84 remain. New drives and changed infill produce additional or shifted samples, so subtracting those figures does not equal the final total.

Legitimate water, protected parks, physical compounds, road junctions and station approaches are separate categories in the underlying survey. No unresolved ray is relabeled a park merely because there is green terrain.
