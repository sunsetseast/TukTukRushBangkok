# Visual fidelity review

Grades follow the requested scale: A = clearly recognizable and convincing; B = recognizable but simplified; C = weak/incomplete resemblance; D = name-only placeholder. **No A grades are claimed.** These are a manual art review, not proof from model names or geometry tests. The source catalog links actual official/architect photographs. Every installed real property has road and elevated renders; hero assets also have front, skyline and applicable river views.

Five of the eleven hotel destinations pass B in this draft: Peninsula, Millennium Hilton, Lebua/State Tower, Capella and Rosewood. The other six are integrated architectural studies and remain partial. The requested 10–12 faithfully recognizable completed hotels has **not** been achieved. This report does not count partial hotels as completed.

## Hero evidence

The comparison page places actual exterior-reference links alongside the in-game views. Road shots use the normal chase camera at a documented connected street/drop-off setup; front/elevated/river/skyline shots use explicitly reported QA camera positions. QA viewpoints are not advertised as normal driving views.

[Hero comparison catalog](comparisons.md) · [Source/provenance catalog](references.md) · [Raw camera and rendering survey](after/survey.json)

Normal driving intentionally views the street at tuk-tuk height; tall roofs may be above the driving frame. Existing LOOK presets frame the full architecture and now use the new properties’ actual registered heights. This does not waive the requirement to make lower entrance/street geometry visible.

Important remaining environmental issue: several riverfront parcels still have broad, sparsely developed lawns between their architecture and the river. Added garden pockets and piers improve context, but this is not the fully finished luxury-hotel landscape or dense Bangkok river district requested. Residual street and context gaps are disclosed rather than hidden in presentation views.
