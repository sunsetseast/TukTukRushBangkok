# Manual architectural and geographic scorecard

Grades are review judgments from real reference photos, actual ground/elevated/normal driving captures, data and route tests. B = recognizable with explicit compressed geometry; C = useful but materially incomplete; D = major deficiency. Passing code tests does not imply an A. The final gallery is the evidence, not an automatic grading algorithm.

Palace roofs and walls read clearly at the north gate, but the miniature does not reproduce every ceremonial courtyard or guarded gate. Sanam is a recognizable major protected field; adjacent institutions remain an interpretation rather than individually reconstructed buildings. Wat Pho retains its Buddha, chedis and halls, but the west approach is partially blocked by an inherited shophouse, and entrances/precincts are simplified.

The Swing is prominent with Suthat behind the public road; this is the strongest relative-placement correction. Golden Mount now reads as a temple hill with stairs and lower buildings, but terrace details and roof proportions remain stylized. Democracy gains the missing circulating setting, but Ratchadamnoen's facade rhythm and avenue orientation remain approximate. Hua's established facade remains intact; the operational yard is heavily shortened. Traimit's white/gold mondop and stairs are identifiable, but exact compound orientation and surrounding individual parcels need further reference work. Odeon retains its recognizable gate and one-way Yaowarat while adding the missing station–temple connection; full Chinatown is outside scope.
