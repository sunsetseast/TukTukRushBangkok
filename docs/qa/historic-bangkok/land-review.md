# Empty-space and frontage review

The survey uses the same 1,014 points on a 36-game-metre grid in the same selected region before and after. Water, public roads, public spaces and existing authored parcels take precedence over nearby buildings. Baseline uses committed PR31 parcels, not newly enlarged reservations. These are area samples, not the earlier citywide 812 roadside-sample metric; they must not be compared as the same denominator.

| Class | Before | After | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| River / canal | 6 | 6 | Retained water, not development |
| Roadway | 121 | 146 | Reconstructed perimeter, circle and connections |
| Park / public space | 32 | 75 | Sanam's outer public grounds now protected |
| Authored reservation | 53 | 98 | Temple courts, plazas, forecourts and station yard |
| Developed | 699 | 677 | Dense nearby frontage; houses removed from authentic open grounds |
| Unclassified vacant | 103 | 12 | Residual candidate gaps, not automatically legitimate lots |

Selected-region buildings increase 867 → 973, while citywide buildings increase 6,965 → 7,091. The vacancy reduction is **not** 91 new buildings or proof that 91 missing parcels were developed. It combines actual infill with more accurate classification and protection. `land-before.json`, `land-after.json` and the [classification map](land-classification.png) preserve all samples.

| Reviewed area | Classification and treatment |
|---|---|
| Sanam Luang | Ceremonial field, public paved perimeter, tree boundary. Preserve original lawn; remove houses inside public grounds. |
| Palace / Pho / Suthat | Walled sacred compounds, pedestrian courts and public approaches; no vehicle shortcuts or generic infill. |
| Swing square / Democracy island | Civic paved spaces, not vacant commercial lots. Surrounding blocks carry frontage. |
| Golden Mount | Solid landscaped hill, shaded terraces, lower temple courts; no procedural buildings on the hill. |
| Hua Lamphong | Preserved passenger forecourt plus ground railway reservation north; not generic missing development. |
| Traimit / Odeon | Temple court and stairs, gateway island and traffic approaches; infill stays on surrounding street-facing parcels. |
| Connecting streets | Existing varied Old Town and Chinese-Thai shophouse grammar, doors, balconies, awnings, roofs and signs; expanded bounded generation, not arbitrary off-street blocks. |
| Residual 12 samples | Remain explicitly unclassified. Small setbacks, service gaps and parcel-edge ambiguities require individual research before more infill; full cadastral completeness is not claimed. |

No invented surveyed parcel line is presented. A coarse grid cannot certify every lot or prove every facade is historically accurate. The elevated gallery makes remaining street-edge gaps and compressed institutions visible.
