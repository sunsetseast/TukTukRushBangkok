# Sources, licences and interpretation

Research checked the following site descriptions and existing geographic evidence while preparing this change. Nominal WGS84 centers are approximate reference points, not surveys. Real reference maps show centers only; newly authored game road traces and compound rectangles are explicitly game design controls.

| Source | Verified observation used | Design interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| [Royal Grand Palace](https://www.royalgrandpalace.th/en/home) and its official visitor/map material | Palace east of river, public north approach, Sanam north, Pho south | Enlarged existing model, rectangular miniature grounds, public Na Phra Lan stop |
| [Wat Pho](https://www.watpho.com/en/) | Neighboring Palace and Tha Tien, walled temple with distinct halls/chedis | Retained original Buddha and halls, compact southeast compound |
| [TAT Wat Suthat](https://www.tourismthailand.org/Attraction/wat-suthatthepwararam-ratchaworamahawihan), [OSM way 178353988](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/178353988) | Swing north of Suthat, both east of Palace; nominal Swing latitude north of Palace | Feature origin separated from combined asset; compressed public square |
| [TAT Wat Saket](https://www.tourismthailand.org/Attraction/wat-saket-and-the-golden-mount) | Temple hill with summit chedi, lower grounds and stair access | Original simplified terraces, shortened stair and hill proportions |
| [TAT Democracy Monument](https://www.tourismthailand.org/Attraction/democracy-monument) | Civic monument at Ratchadamnoen/Dinso | 38m arcade road circle, four graph arms, diagonal avenue retained |
| [TAT Wat Traimit](https://www.tourismthailand.org/Attraction/wat-traimit-withayaram) and real licensed photographs | White/gold mondop with raised stairs near Chinatown gateway | South-facing compact building and compressed compound, not traced footprint |
| [TAT Yaowarat](https://www.tourismthailand.org/Attraction/yaowarat-road) | Odeon as Chinatown gateway; Chinese-Thai commercial character | Existing Gate/Yaowarat retained, connecting Tri Mit bent around temple |
| [State Railway of Thailand](https://www.railway.co.th/) and retained PR31 station reference photos | Mainline station's south arched frontage and rear train sheds; distinct underground MRT | Existing facade preserved, shortened north yard; no elevated MRT |
| [OSM Sanam Luang way 23630232](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/23630232) and [TAT Sanam Luang context](https://www.tatnews.org/2025/04/tat-announces-kawsholiday-thailand-at-sanam-luang/) | Major ceremonial open space north of Palace | Retain inherited normalized lawn boundary; manually authored outer public perimeter |

Inherited OSM-derived reference snapshots retain [ODbL attribution](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) in the earlier geographic QA report. Public Overpass endpoints did not provide a reliable fresh extract in this environment; this PR does not claim fresh surveyed road or footprint data. Road names and relative approaches are researched; every bend, width, game distance and local compound boundary remains an authored compression.

[Photograph attribution and licences](references/ATTRIBUTION.md) link the Commons original pages, photographers and CC terms. Photos are resized only and used in review documents, not shipped runtime textures. Prior PR31 photos remain linked in their existing directory. No AI-generated comparison art is used.
