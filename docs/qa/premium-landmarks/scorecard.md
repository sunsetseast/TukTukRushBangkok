# Critical visual scorecard

Grades assess resemblance, composition and public context independently of automated engineering passes. A means convincing premium production quality across the normal approaches; B means recognizable and substantially improved with visible simplifications; C means useful progress but material context/proportion/arrival deficiencies; D means largely generic or obstructed; F means broken or misidentified. No site receives A.

This is the implementing author's critical reference/WebGL review, **not an independent human certification**. An external art/geography review is still required. Source photographs are linked in [references](sources.md); compare them with all matched [game views](gallery.md), including the weak/cropped views. Exact gates and curb operation are not inferred from a centre coordinate.

Across the six sites, materials gain facade depth and selective warm room/sign illumination. The game still uses stylized opaque glass rather than reflection-heavy glazing. Night views retain atmospheric warmth but lack the full diversity of real street activity and public-realm lighting. Nearby modern parcels now have visible recessed shells, retail bases and four controlled facade variants; some repetition remains.

The priority improvements are real and visible, but these grades deliberately leave the unfinished premium acceptance gates open.
