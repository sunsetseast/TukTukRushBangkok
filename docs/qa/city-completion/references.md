# Research and architectural decisions

Checked 18 October 2026. Primary project/operator sources guide original geometric interpretations. Reference photography is not bundled as a runtime texture. None of these sources certify this game's fidelity.

| Source | Applied decision | Remaining simplification |
|---|---|---|
| [Terminal 21 operator map/contact](https://www.terminal21.co.th/asok/contactus/th) | Mall/hotel northwest of Asok, north of Sukhumvit; shared anchor moves map, missions, physical mass, arrival and station link | The inherited diagonal Sukhumvit requires an exaggerated northward setback; MRT concourses and intersection streetscape remain abbreviated |
| [LINEHOUSE CentralWorld project](https://linehousedesign.com/project/central-world-2/) | Retain overlapping arches, terraces and greenery; add connected northern hotel context and integrated display band | Department-store renovation is only part of the real complex; this is not a complete measured master plan |
| [Apple Central World, Foster + Partners](https://www.fosterandpartners.com/projects/apple-central-world) | Round glazed pavilion and broad floating roof in the east event square | Original low-poly pavilion and tree column; interior/spiral stair omitted |
| [CentralWorld operator](https://www.centralworld.co.th/en) | Event-square frontage faces east toward Ratchadamri | Road topology/metric parcel size compressed |
| [MBK Center](https://mbk-center.co.th/en/) | Horizontal retail mass, rooftop barrel, articulated metal shoulder and signage | Full facade detail and National Stadium pedestrian network not reproduced |
| [Siam Center](https://www.siamcenter.co.th/en) and [Siam Square](https://www.siamsquare.co.th/) | Shaded low commercial mass, deep storefront frames and projecting entrance; two/three-storey retail/café/shophouse fabric south of Rama I | Facade screens are an original stylized adaptation; full individual storefront inventory is not copied |
| [Turenscape Benjakitti Forest Park](https://www.turenscape.com/en/project/detail/4751.html) | Original lake east; three wetland fields west; islet mosaics, boardwalks, canopy skywalk, reused factory sheds | 28 islands represent a much larger real islet system; no assertion of a surveyed outline. Convention-centre relationship and Green Mile need further work |
| [Wat Pho architecture](https://watpho.com/index.php/en/architecture) and [official plan](https://www.watpho.com/th/contact/plan) | Retained Reclining Buddha pavilion, expanded hall/ubosot hierarchy, cloisters, gates, walls and circulation | Deliberately compressed sacred precinct; many chedis, halls and decorative details remain omitted |
| [Thai government Wat Suthat record](https://www.thailandtourismdirectory.go.th/en/attraction/103311) | Roof hierarchy, main hall south of Giant Swing, terrace details | Civic surroundings and precise ornamental sculpture remain incomplete |
| [Jim Thompson foundation](https://jimthompsonhouse.org/foundation/) | Preserve six raised teak houses, strengthen mature garden canopy | Botanically simplified; surrounding skyline context still needs visual review |
| [River City visitor information](https://rivercitybangkok.com/plan-your-visit/) | Retain corrected river-facing facade, add surrounding service/heritage blocks and nearby pier | Pier models are generic compressed passenger infrastructure, not surveyed reproductions |

The inherited hotel compounds and 7-Eleven artwork remain intact. New context uses district rules rather than replacing those authored models. [PR27 references](../geographic-city-reconstruction/references.md) remain the baseline for retained work.
