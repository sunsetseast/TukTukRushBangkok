# Architectural fidelity review

Grades apply to rendered architecture, not code coverage. A = highly recognizable and convincingly faithful; B = recognizable with reasonable simplification; C = partial/generic in important respects; D = inadequate. **No A grades are claimed.** C assets below are partial implementations and are not counted as completed faithful buildings.

The initial rendered pass exposed a buried Oriental facade. Its faceted glass profile and outward floor bands were corrected, verified with a surface-ray regression test, and photographed again. A second review broadened Four Seasons' parcel and cascading wings, differentiated Shangri-La's rounded main wing from the deep-balcony Krungthep wing, and added planted islands with real collision to sparse river terraces.

| Asset | Grade | Result and limitations |
|---|---|---|
| Mandarin Oriental | B | Faceted River Wing, separate smaller Garden Wing, pitched-roof Authors' Wing with arches/shutters/pediment, restaurant and river terrace are clearly distinct. Heritage ornament, rear elevation and full garden composition remain simplified. |
| Shangri-La | B | Broad rounded main wing and smaller deeply balconied Krungthep wing, connecting lobby, crown setbacks, river pavilions and pool. The exact sweeping main-wing plan and all facade elevations remain compressed. |
| Four Seasons | B | Much broader cascade, separate lower Palm Courtyard buildings, multiple actual open courts, planted terraces and reflecting pools. Exact asymmetric court dimensions and every terrace recess are not replicated. |
| Capella | B | Room wing, deep balcony rhythm, separate court edges and three river villas with small pools; much smaller than the neighboring cascade. Villa count and landscape layout are compressed. |
| Mahanakhon | B | Narrower body, shallower clustered cuts, one quieter staggered ribbon and identifiable sky deck. Pixel layout is not a floor-by-floor reconstruction. |
| Central Embassy / Park Hyatt | C | Continuous rounded retail loop, curved hotel floor bands and rising white ribbon replace separate rectangular ribbons. The crown currently reads too much like an open arch, and the actual asymmetric silver envelope/void proportions are still insufficiently faithful. Another dedicated shell-modeling pass is required. |
| Siam Paragon | B | Curved glazed entrance jewel, gold frame, stepped retail shoulders and plaza-facing frontage. The entire mall plan, signage composition and exact BTS interface are simplified. |
| CentralWorld | C | Longer differentiated frontage, deeper fins, asymmetric shoulders and folded event-entry canopy; full-scale long facade, CentralWorld Square and complete skywalk network remain incomplete. |
| ICONSIAM | B, retained | Existing folded river-facing roof/glass composition and drivable arrival retained; explicit site anchor replaces runtime plot fitting. Adjacent residential towers and full riverside plaza remain incomplete. |
| Park Ventures / Okura | B | Chamfered blue glass, diagonal facade bracing and large rounded pool cantilever distinguish the mixed-use tower. The complete stepped crown and full BTS pedestrian connection are simplified. |
| Anantara Riverside | B | Long low-rise Thai-roof guest wings, pool court, separate riverside pavilions and planted terrace read as a resort, with a coherent west-bank road approach. Garden canopy is still sparser than the real resort; full pier/restaurant plan is condensed. |
| Dusit Thani / Central Park | C | Rebuilt park-facing hotel with restored spire, office mass and terraced retail roof garden are physically integrated. Hotel body and office tower remain too generic; the exact stepped crown, bay proportions and complete roof park need further modeling. |
| One Bangkok / Ritz-Carlton | C | Multi-building district in the correct Lumphini relationship, three different tower masses, retail blocks and public court. Individual tower silhouettes, full retail streets and master-plan proportions are not faithful enough for B. Future supertall phase deliberately omitted. |

Retained supporting architecture: Peninsula, Hilton, Lebua/State Tower, Rosewood, Baiyoke, MBK and Jim Thompson keep their useful PR25 forms. Erawan, Eastin, River City, Siam Center/Discovery and Terminal 21 remain supporting studies with the limitations in the [PR25 review](../real-bangkok-architecture/visual-review.md); this PR does not claim new fidelity completion for them.

## Evidence and camera interpretation

[Reference inventory](references.md) · [Before/after comparisons](comparisons.md) · [Raw capture survey](after/survey.json)

`road` images use the normal chase camera after a documented setup at an actual connected approach. `elevated`, `river`, `front` and `night` use explicit QA cameras. These are actual WebGL renders, not concept art. The cameras do not remove occluding buildings. Some street/alternate shots therefore show restricted visibility; this is part of the player-experience evidence, not hidden by deleting scenery.

The stable capture pass uses 800 × 500; final proportional reviews for Shangri-La, Four Seasons and Capella use 1000 × 650. Browser controller and map checks use 844 × 390 and 390 × 844. Physical iPhone Safari performance is unverified.

The scene still has broad sparse areas beyond individual compound boundaries. Planted islands improve terraces, but this is not a certification of a fully finished Bangkok or the brief's all-hero A/strong-B target. The C grades are explicit outstanding work.
