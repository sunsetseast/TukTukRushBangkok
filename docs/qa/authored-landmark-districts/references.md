# Reference inventory and geographic decisions

Research: 17 October 2026. Original source-built meshes only. Photographs are linked for analysis, never copied into runtime assets. Coordinates below describe the condensed game map, not a GPS projection. The [PR25 source catalog](../real-bangkok-architecture/references.md) remains the supporting inventory for retained architecture.

| Building | Primary photographic / geographic references | Architectural decisions and coverage |
|---|---|---|
| Mandarin Oriental | [Official gallery](https://www.mandarinoriental.com/en/bangkok/chao-phraya-river/gallery), [Authors' Wing garden exterior](https://media.ffycdn.net/eu/mandarin-oriental-hotel-group/6rjmEQVuUXB55kFkkCsr.jpg), [official wing location map](https://photos.mandarinoriental.com/is/content/MandarinOriental/bangkok-guestroom-location-map), [P&T photographic archive](https://www.mplus.org.hk/en/collection/archives/drawings-and-photographic-materials-oriental-hotel-now-mandarin-oriental-19731976-bang-rak-bangkok-thailand-ca71-9/) | East bank, Oriental Avenue. Distinct River/Garden/Authors wings, cream framed faceted river bays, green roof and ornamental heritage pediment, arched openings/shutters, terrace restaurant. Sources cover garden approach, river/wing arrangement and historical alternate elevations. Exact modern rear elevation remains less well documented. |
| Shangri-La | [Official hotel](https://www.shangri-la.com/bangkok/shangrila/), [official wing/guest-services plan](https://www.shangri-la.com/-/media/Shangri-La/bangkok_shangrila/about/20240417_SLBK_GuestServicesDirectory_EN.pdf), [Krungthep Wing description](https://www.shangri-la.com/en/bangkok/shangrila/rooms-suites/rooms/krungthep-wing/about-the-krungthep-wing/) | East bank, south of Oriental, near Saphan Taksin. Broad taller main wing, lower deep-balcony Krungthep wing, pools and Thai river pavilions. Plan and riverfront coverage; official image access restricted for some angles. Street/rear reference coverage remains incomplete. |
| Four Seasons | [Official hotel](https://www.fourseasons.com/bangkok/), [architectural facts](https://press.fourseasons.com/bangkok/hotel-facts/), [design article](https://press.fourseasons.com/bangkok/trending-now/hotel-design-and-artworks) | East bank, 300/1 Charoen Krung, adjacent to Capella. Distinguish 12-floor Cascade Building, four-floor Palm Courtyard and unrelated 73-floor residences. Cascading side wings, open reflecting courts, planted roof terraces. Official courtyard/river/gallery perspectives; condensed wing lengths and exact rear composition remain simplified. |
| Central Embassy / Park Hyatt | [AL_A project and photographic set](https://www.ala.uk.com/selected-projects/central-embassy/), [street/skyline](https://www.ala.uk.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Bangkok-home-page-768x486.jpg), [aerial side](https://www.ala.uk.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Drone_Building-sideview_fixed-768x1025.jpg), [Hyatt design description](https://newsroom.hyatt.com/2012-02-17-HYATT-AND-CENTRAL-EMBASSY-ANNOUNCE-PLANS-FOR-ICONIC-PARK-HYATT-BANGKOK-TWISTED-COIL-DESIGN-TO-BE-SKYLINE-STANDOUT) | Phloen Chit / Wireless Road. Integrated silver retail loop and upper hotel, rounded continuous floor bands, climbing white skin and crown light-well. Street, elevated side and alternate AL_A gallery angles. Tile micro-pattern is omitted; geometry controls the silhouette. |
| Mahanakhon | [Büro Ole Scheeren project/photos/drawings](https://www.buro-os.com/projects/mahanakhon), [Bouygues](https://www.bouygues-construction.com/en/sectors/luxury/luxury-residential/mahanakhon-tower) | Silom / Chong Nonsi. Narrower body; clustered, shallow perimeter cuts replace the overlarge uniform double helix. Shared near/far occupancy, restrained blue curtain wall and sky deck. Project gallery supplies multiple exterior angles; compressed pixel rhythm is not a floor-by-floor replica. |
| Dusit Thani / Central Park | [Official hotel](https://www.dusit.com/dusitthani-bangkok/), [owner's restored-spire announcement](https://www.dusit.com/a-legendary-landmark-returns-the-iconic-dusit-thani-bangkok-announces-september-reopening/), [development design concept](https://dusitcentralpark.com/design-concept/), [official gallery](https://www.dusit.com/dusitthani-bangkok/gallery/) | 98 Rama IV, southwest of Lumphini Park. Rebuilt hotel represented, not demolished original. Park-facing bays, restored gold spire, separate office mass and stepped retail roof planting. Owner location and development/roofline references; full as-built reverse elevation coverage remains incomplete. |
| One Bangkok / Ritz-Carlton | [SOM master plan](https://www.som.com/projects/one-bangkok/), [owner location](https://www.onebangkok.com/en/location/), [Ritz-Carlton exterior](https://www.onebangkok.com/en/hospitality/the-ritz-carlton-bangkok/), [hotel photo gallery](https://www.ritzcarlton.com/en/hotels/bkkrb-the-ritz-carlton-bangkok/photos/) | Wireless / Rama IV, east of Lumphini. Compressed five-part district with Ritz-Carlton/residential mass, two distinct office towers, retail blocks, public court and Wireless House context. Future supertall phase is deliberately omitted. SOM direct page returned 403 during one retrieval; owner's current pages supply location/photography. Exact tower spacing and complete master plan remain simplified. |
| Park Ventures / Okura | [P&T project](https://web.p-t-group.com/en/project-detail.php?projects_category_id=2&projects_id=64&projects_name=Park+Ventures+Ecoplex), [approach/upper cantilever](https://web.p-t-group.com/uploaded_files/projects_image/64/475/B4297_01.jpg), [opposite elevated facade](https://web.p-t-group.com/uploaded_files/projects_image/64/476/B4297_02.jpg), [corner facade](https://web.p-t-group.com/uploaded_files/projects_image/64/477/B4297_03.jpg), [hotel](https://www.okurabangkok.com/) | South side of Phloen Chit, near Wireless Road. Chamfered blue glass shaft, pale horizontal bands, diagonal bracing, roof accents and a large rounded pool cantilever. All three architect photographs inspected; pool is integrated into the mixed-use tower. |
| Anantara Riverside | [Official resort / west-bank statement](https://www.anantara.com/en/riverside-bangkok), [official resort plan](https://www.anantara.com/uploads/minor/anantara/documents/anantara-riverside-bangkok-resort/hotel-info/hotel-information-2025/anantara_riverside_resort_bangkok_resort_map_280125.pdf), [official exterior](https://assets.anantara.com/image/upload/q_auto,f_auto/media/minor/anantara/images/anantara-riverside-bangkok-resort/the-resort/main/anantara_riverside_bangkok_exterior_daylight_880x620.jpg) | Charoen Nakhon, west bank, opposite/south of Asiatique. Low guest wings, Thai roofs, planted pool court, separate restaurants. Plan, elevated/exterior and resort gallery views available. Extended existing west-bank street network instead of routing a driveway over the river. Exact full garden/pier plan remains condensed. |
| Capella | [Owner / aerial](https://capellahotels.com/en/capella-bangkok), [aerial photo](https://capellahotels.com/assets/img/site_images/bangkok/Capella-Bangkok-Top-01.jpg) | Adjacent to Four Seasons, east bank. Room wing, deeper balconies, separate low courts and river villas with small pools. Aerial and river hotel photography; reduced villa count. |
| ICONSIAM | [Facade engineer seele](https://seele.com/project/glass-front-iconsiam-bangkok), [sedak](https://www.sedak.com/en/architecture/facade-glass/iconsiam-bangkok-thailand/) | Preserve PR24/25 folded glass/roof composition and west-bank arrival loop. Explicit authored anchor replaces runtime fitting. Existing residential towers remain omitted. |
| Paragon / CentralWorld | [Paragon](https://www.siamparagon.co.th/about), [CentralWorld](https://centralworld.co.th/en) | Rama I / Ratchaprasong. Paragon curved entrance glazing and broad shoulders; CentralWorld asymmetric retail fronts, deeper fins and event-entry canopy. Commercial facade studies; exact whole-mall footprints and full pedestrian skywalk network remain incomplete. |

Retained/refined properties use PR25's official references: Peninsula (W plan and crown), Hilton (rounded slab and rooftop disc), State Tower/Lebua (pale setbacks and dome), Rosewood (KPF wai profiles), Erawan, Eastin, River City, Siam Center/Discovery, Jim Thompson, MBK, Terminal 21 and Baiyoke. No new completion grade is inferred solely from retaining a model or adding a name.
