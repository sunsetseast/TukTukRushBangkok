Current main: `9e24ada` (merged PR26). Regenerated authored-site audit: 25 sites, 17 real-place compounds, 47 solid masses, 77 unresolved street-edge rays / 8,534 samples. Baseline output is committed alongside this audit; historical PR26 reports are preserved.

- Lumphini and Benjakitti are rectangular protected obstacles. Their landmark renderer returns an empty group; environmental pockets do not describe real lakes, gates, recreation grounds or circulation. Generic global green ground makes these defects hard to distinguish from ordinary undeveloped blocks.
- Victory's Phaya Thai/Phahonyothin road runs through the design anchor. A nearby-fit landmark algorithm moves the monument onto a square parcel rather than planning a traffic island. Existing one-way routing/yield infrastructure can support an authored clockwise ring.
- Sanam Luang has no authored ceremonial-field outline or perimeter treatment. It needs explicit legitimate open-space classification rather than infill.
- Giant Swing/Wat Suthat is compressed into a tiny slab. Flowers are repeated small sheds. Jim Thompson lacks a convincing compound/canal lane. River City is an isolated single mass.
- MBK, Paragon, CentralWorld and Terminal21 inherit small generic mall reservations. Center/Discovery share a legacy discovery identifier and need distinct physical forms. Widen parcels deliberately rather than scaling long malls into squares.
- Urban-completeness already adds real collision-backed frontage and rear buildings, but coverage remains shallow and global lawn material dominates block interiors. Repair physical parcels and actual service courts; do not relabel unexplained grass as parks.
- Preserve usable PR26 hotel geometry, riverbanks/bridges, destination identities, controller/boost, left-side traffic, transit, discovery and save systems. Intentional civic street changes must update rendering, routing, transit supports and reservations together.

Implementation priorities: canonical sourced geography; authored public spaces with gates/lakes/parking and protected exclusions; Victory road reconstruction; historic/retail districts; modular real 7-Eleven identity; urban fabric; actual rendered/driver/performance comparisons. Exact-scale whole-city reconstruction is outside the condensed game map; compromises and partial fidelity grades must remain explicit.
