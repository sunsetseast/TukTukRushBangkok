# Rendered fidelity review

Reviewed against the linked architectural/geographic references and the actual before/after WebGL gallery. These are self-review grades, not independent architectural certification. **No A grades are awarded.** A B identifies the represented signature form or geography; the notes explicitly limit that judgment. Several rebuilt models and districts remain C and are not declared complete.

A = highly recognizable, convincing proportions/context. B = recognizable defining architecture/geography with reasonable simplification. C = partial, generic or geographically compromised. D = inadequate/incorrect.

## What the rendered review changed

The initial River City draft repeated a glazed arch that the current facade photograph did not support. It was replaced with the screened, staggered horizontal waterfront facade before final capture. Retail colliders were corrected after inspection found that older arrival logic was overwriting the new rectangular mall footprints with square obstacles. Discovery/Center orientation was corrected so Discovery is west and both face Rama I. Capella was corrected to lie north of Four Seasons.

The QA approach setup initially carried chase-camera history between teleported capture locations. The capture harness now invalidates that history through the same revision mechanism used by restart. Night views start from an actual road approach; the Lumphini landscape camera is on a park bridge, and the Wireless entrance view starts at the parking mouth instead of looking diagonally through the adjacent street block. These are documented camera corrections, not scenery deletion. Normal road views remain the actual chase camera. No building is hidden for a screenshot.

## Acceptance limits

The requested citywide finished-terrain standard is **not met**. The unresolved roadside audit improves from 77/8,534 to 71/9,546 samples, while behind-frontage and waterfront gaps remain visible. The change from green base terrain to mineral ground is an art-direction correction and is not counted as filled development. The protected ceremonial lawn and actual park polygons are legitimate open uses.

Victory is slightly west of Siam in the compressed game despite the real monument being slightly east; Terminal 21 remains on the wrong side of the compressed Asok intersection. Both are explicit geographic debts. Full low-rise Siam Square, CentralWorld master plan, exact historic civic context, dense flower-market blocks, mature museum gardens, Khlong San infill and Benjakitti forest/wetlands are deferred rather than filled with placeholders.

The screenshots use Chromium software WebGL. They show actual game meshes and materials. Native emulated-touch testing and deterministic controller drives verify access, but no physical iPhone Safari test or independent human playthrough is claimed.
