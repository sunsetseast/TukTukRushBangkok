# Siam and Asok commercial reconstruction

Research and model audit: 18 October 2026. Reference images are analysed only; none is shipped as a texture or hotlinked by the game. The new facade lettering is original geometric text, not copied commercial graphics.

## Baseline audit

| Asset | Main baseline defect | Reconstruction decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MBK | Almost square 61 × 55 shopping box, three billboard panels; weak long-mall proportions | Replace with 120 × 65 reserved site, elongated rounded horizontal ribbon block, four large display bays, north/east commercial entry facade and raised concourse |
| Siam Discovery | Uniform 4 × 6 grid on one half of a shared retail block | Rebuild as a separate transparent retail volume with large varied frames, a finer irregular white outline second skin and wrapping corner detail |
| Siam Center | Dark box joined to Discovery with no convincing independent frontage | Separate lower dark glass volume with tall display bays, recessed glass entrance, distinct cornice and name lettering |
| Siam Paragon | Small half-cylinder entrance plus narrow block shoulders | Expand to a broad mall, low half-elliptical glazed entrance drum and domed cap, gold ribs, terraced shoulders and fountain forecourt |
| CentralWorld | Short three-box frontage, regular glass grid and small red canopy; grade C in PR26 | Replace with a long asymmetrical retail composition, separate stepped shoulders, large glazed middle, overlapping shaded arches on department-store end, central event plaza and retail display facade |
| Terminal 21 | Squared hotel slab and repetitive glass podium do not reflect actual tower plan | Replace with Y-plan hotel, rounded glazed vertical bay noses, gold triangular center/crown, rounded corner shopping glazing, silver horizontal podium, rooftop pool and planted recreation deck |

Existing discovery IDs are preserved. `siam-center` still identifies the combined Center/Discovery destination for old saves; the two malls have different geometry and individual visible facade names. Discovery is authored at local negative X (west), Center at positive X (east), and the shared +Z shopping frontage faces Rama I to the south with site yaw zero. Geometry and text retain positive scales and outward normals. Geographic anchors, roads, reservations and approach routing are handled by the city reconstruction foundation rather than by a runtime mall placement search.

## Geometry and mobile safeguards

All new forms are procedural original meshes. Opaque glass-colored shared materials avoid sorting costs and transparency overdraw. Each model has near/far geometry, whole-landmark frustum/distance culling and only three to five batched mesh submissions per LOD. Repeated facade details and geometric sign letters are merged into existing material batches, not individual Three.js draw calls. There are no new runtime textures, downloaded photographs, GLBs or dependencies. Planting and fountains are low geometry; there are no per-store or per-window point lights.

The code exports `COMMERCIAL_DISTRICT_BOUNDS` as a placement contract. Enlarged native dimensions are not scaled down by `Landmark.size`. Reservations/collisions must be generated from the corresponding authored site dimensions, so a wide mall does not render outside a square physical block.

## Remaining limitations

The work improves public silhouettes, facade depth and distinct mall identities. It does not certify exact architectural plans, exact current tenant displays, complete interior retail, the full MBK mixed-use towers, all Siam car parks or every CentralWorld tower. Street-height, alternate and night captures in the main approval gallery are the final evidence; source-model budgets alone do not imply architectural approval. No physical iPhone test is claimed.
