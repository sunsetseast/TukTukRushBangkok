## Evidence limits

OSM was retrieved as selected vector coordinates through its public API. The stored snapshots carry source URLs, retrieval date and ODbL attribution. They contain approximate mapped centers and selected rings, not a navigation-grade survey. A direct Overpass request was unavailable; no claim is made that a full Bangkok municipal dataset was imported.

Official websites supplied public-access descriptions, facade photographs and neighborhood addresses; where precise service gates and reverse elevations were unavailable, the model uses a stated gameplay simplification. The screenshot gallery distinguishes normal chase-camera road views from fixed inspection cameras. Grades belong to rendered geometry and actual context, not to the quantity of links listed here.
