Grades are visual review judgments, not outputs of collision tests. No A grades are awarded.

## Recognition without labels

- **Palace:** recognizable at the principal public approaches from white fortified walls, ornate layered roofs and stepped crowns. Internal ceremonial hierarchy and roof proportions remain compressed. A low river camera is not guaranteed an unobstructed royal skyline.
- **Wat Pho:** likely identifiable from the major chedis and temple roofs; individual west-gate and courtyard architecture still needs more site-specific work. The corrected gate is not enough to certify the entire compound.
- **River context:** reads as a dense historic Bangkok commercial river edge, but exact Tha Tien property identity is not reliable without a label. Bay repetition, bank compression and individually unmodeled civic properties keep this at C for architectural accuracy.
- **Golden Mount:** recognizable as a gold-crowned landscaped temple hill rather than a plain green mound. Terraces, stairs and lower halls are still a stylized approximation rather than a walkable replica of every level.
- **Traimit:** recognizable white/gold multilevel temple, ceremonial stair and roof crown. Exact orientation and compound subdivisions remain approximate.
- **Democracy:** recognizable four-wing monument within its functional clockwise intersection. Avenue facades and their continuous civic rhythm remain less specific than the monument.
- **Swing / Suthat:** recognizable red Swing and temple relationship, preserved from PR32. Narrow crossbeam ray misses do not imply an invisible landmark or justify deleting adjacent shops.
- **Sanam Luang:** recognizable open ceremonial field with shaded tamarind perimeter; nearby individual institutions and precise canopy ages remain approximate.
- **Hua Lamphong:** recognizable south facade, clock and barrel-roof silhouette preserved. The west secondary approach is partially blocked and the rail yard remains shortened.
- **Gate corridor:** recognizable Chinese gateway and Odeon relationship, with the retained one-way Yaowarat connection. Secondary commercial facades are district grammar rather than property replicas.

Night grades indicate readable silhouettes and continuity with the existing sunset/night cycle, not surveyed lighting fixtures. Driving grades indicate route accessibility; the separate traffic collision caveat remains in [testing](testing.md). The photographs and normal driving views in the gallery are the evidence supporting these grades.
