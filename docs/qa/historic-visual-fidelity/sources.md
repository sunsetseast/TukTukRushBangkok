# Research and photographic attribution

Research informs composition and building type. It does not establish a surveyed parcel for each procedural building. The world remains deliberately compressed; map metres are game coordinates, not geographic measurements.

| Primary reference | Application and limits |
|---|---|
| [Royal Grand Palace architecture](https://www.royalgrandpalace.th/en/discover/architecture) | White fortified perimeter, distinct royal/temple roofs, Chakri prasat crowns and compound hierarchy; ornamental details are simplified. |
| [Wat Pho official site](https://www.watpho.com/en) | Temple precinct, chedis and cloister/gate relationships. The new west approach is a compressed adaptation, not a measured replica. |
| [Museum Siam/National Archives Tha Tien material](https://museumlinks.museumsiam.org/view-resource.php?l_id=67) | Historic commercial and riverside context rather than an empty boulevard. |
| [Princess Maha Chakri Sirindhorn Anthropology Centre: Tha Tien community](https://wikicommunity.sac.or.th/community/1890) | Commercial community and river-access context. |
| [Museum Siam Bangkok shophouses catalogue](https://repository.museumsiam.org/items/b9120587-c307-4aab-95a5-58b2e85eff58) | Catalogue metadata was checked; the full book was not treated as newly inspected evidence. |
| [BMA Sanam Luang tamarind planting](https://invest.bangkok.go.th/green-space-mark-duplicate-30-oct-2025/) | Species character for perimeter planting. Individual tree positions/ages are approximate. |
| [Tourism Authority of Thailand](https://www.tourismthailand.org/) | Golden Mount and Golden Buddha temple identity/context; photographs below govern the simplified silhouettes. |
| [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/) | Geographic cross-reference inherited from PR32; no replacement road graph, new geocoding or OSM runtime dependency. |

## Photographs

References are QA documentation only. They are not used as runtime textures. No AI-generated images are used as implementation evidence. Images were resized/recompressed only; architecture was not altered. Dates, projection, compression and camera positions differ from the game.

The existing Palace, Wat Pho, Golden Mount, Democracy Monument, Swing, Hua Lamphong and Chinatown Gate photos retain their individual author and licence entries in [PR31 attribution](../landmark-accuracy/references/ATTRIBUTION.md). Sanam Luang and Traimit retain [PR32 attribution](../historic-bangkok/references/ATTRIBUTION.md).
