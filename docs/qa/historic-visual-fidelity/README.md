# Historic Bangkok visual fidelity — PR33

This is an architectural and environmental refinement of merged PR32 (`341e02f52b04d07dda7688e2f07b47abc19d44a8`). The 2,089 roads, 2,036 nodes, landmark positions, river geometry and transit remain byte-for-byte equivalent in the world data. It is a stylized, compressed Bangkok, not a surveyed digital twin.

## Implemented changes

- Reserve Wat Pho's western pedestrian forecourt wedge and a narrow Tha Tien pier passage through the existing protected-parcel system. Correct the infill at the failed western approach and open a real gate in the authored perimeter wall. The pavement belongs to the existing ground batch, keeping temple meshes inside their collision envelope.
- Retain dense waterfront commercial blocks; give the bounded Palace–Tha Tien band two-storey cream masonry, arcades, shutters, green accents and articulated service elevations. Collision and rendered facade heights agree. No continuous riverside boulevard was invented.
- Add Tha Chang's landing to the existing pier system and finish Tha Tien's approach, benches and landing surfaces. Preserve river navigation, embankments and bridges.
- Refine Palace fortified white walls, merlons, corner pavilions, internal galleries and stepped prasat crowns. Refine Wat Pho's western gate, cloisters, guardians and ceramic chedis while retaining the reclining-Buddha precinct and four major chedis.
- Rework Golden Mount's retaining terraces to follow the irregular hill, join trees to the terrain, add layered planting and intermediate temple details. Improve Traimit's terrace balustrades, columns, doors and ceremonial stairs; add Democracy Monument's radial paving and relief panels.
- Replace Sanam Luang's generic crowns with instanced tamarind silhouettes and physically joined branches on the existing perimeter planting positions. Preserve the large ceremonial field. Near/far crown counts are bounded; no new vegetation system was added.

The building seed order is preserved before local approach filtering, so unrelated neighborhoods do not reshuffle. The result has 7,084 procedural buildings and 208 protected parcels. All 23 removed and 16 added/reflowed footprints lie inside the bounded western study area. Forty-two commercial footprints remain in the waterfront grammar band. The 49 scene landmarks and all 92 existing registered place IDs remain; Tha Chang adds one place through the existing registry. See [map](riverfront-before-after.png) and [footprint deltas](footprint-deltas.json).

The inherited 36m land-use survey now has 13 unclassified samples versus 12, not a blanket completeness improvement. Two former development samples become protected public access; one near (-882,-670) becomes unclassified after neighboring infill removal. It lies near the Maha Rat road margin and needs a more precise frontage/verge survey. The coarse proximity classifier alone does not justify another building. [Exact changes](landuse-comparison.json).

## Certification limits

The corrected Pho approach improves the original volume score from 18/24 to 19/24. Five inherited secondary approaches retain ordinary dense blocks. Their precise real-world property identities have not been authenticated; retaining plausible context is not certification of exact footprints. A clear volume ray does not prove a visible entrance. The mesh report separately records named features, other landmark surfaces, foreground obstructions and targets with no rendered surface.

No physical iPhone or Safari test was available. Chromium/SwiftShader is comparative evidence only. Traffic-enabled Pho → Hua Lamphong reports 31 collision frames; the identical PR32 control also reports 31. The route completes, but is not certified collision-free in traffic. No merge or deployment is part of this change.
