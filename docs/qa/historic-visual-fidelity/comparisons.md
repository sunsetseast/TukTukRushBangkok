# Real photographs and actual WebGL

All ten requested locations/corridors plus Tha Tien. Real photographs are architectural references, not identical lenses or compression. Before frames are unchanged PR32, with provenance in `before/capture.json`; after frames are the implemented game. Medium quality, 768×432, HUD hidden, city objects retained. [Attribution](sources.md) · [Full gallery](comparisons.html) · [Six obstruction cases](obstructions.md).
