name: Geographic source authority
description: How the source-derived geographic prototype differs from the legacy compressed game.
---
The geographic prototype must retain real source road positions, complete building footprints and identities. Keep the working legacy compressed game separately; its fictional shortcut/art-direction permissions do not authorize reshaping the geographic data.

**Why:** The user's geographic stabilization brief makes source fidelity non-negotiable, including when source records disagree. It prioritizes optimization over a new facade system.

**How to apply:** Optimize derived rendering and bounded residency, classify uncertainty explicitly, and make only evidence-supported display/collision corrections. Do not move source buildings to clear a road, simplify away difficult outlines, hide density to improve benchmarks, or describe estimated-width candidates as surveyed mapping errors.

Measure download/decode, activation, GPU buffers and collision work together. Moving geometry construction offline is not automatically an end-to-end mobile improvement.

**Why:** Full precompiled geometry is materially larger than the footprint source even with indexed, compact attributes; CPU improvements alone cannot establish iPhone readiness.

**How to apply:** Use matched real WebGL views and transfer measurements, retain explicit codec precision checks, and withhold hardware-performance claims without a physical iPhone test.
